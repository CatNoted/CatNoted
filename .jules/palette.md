## 2025-07-26 - Redesigning Sidebar for Cozy and Cozy Spacing

**Learning:** Redesigning sidebars with collapsible sections in low-contrast typography requires cohesive line-heights, comfortable padding, and explicit active indicators to preserve visual hierarchy and light/dark theme aesthetics.
